Welcome to StudentHub Portal

StudentHub is the official digital gateway for university students, faculty, and campus administrators. Designed to streamline academic workflows and foster a vibrant campus community, StudentHub brings together everything you need for a successful academic journey in one place.

Through this portal, students can easily track course schedules, register for upcoming skill-building workshops, access digital library archives, monitor examination results, and manage their personal academic profiles with ease.

Explore StudentHub Features

🎯 Event & Workshop Management

Stay informed about upcoming hands-on technical bootcamps, guest lectures, cultural conventions, and sports meets. Register with a single click and receive calendar reminders.

📊 Student Performance Dashboard

  • Lab Submission Timelines: All lab manuals and code assignments must be submitted to the respective portal folder by Friday 5:00 PM.
  • Identity Cards: Carrying your official university RFID Student ID card is compulsory while inside campus premises, library, and examination halls.
  • Library Book Retention: Students can borrow up to 4 books simultaneously for a maximum duration of 14 days without late renewal fees.
